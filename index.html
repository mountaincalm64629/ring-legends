Camera & touch

Drag anywhere on the arena with a mouse or one finger to rotate the camera 360°. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Reset view (or the 0 key) returns to the side camera. On iPad and phones, buttons appear on screen: movement on the left, attacks on the right. Air moves have dashed borders: tap Jump first.

Rules

Best of 3 rounds, 60 seconds each. Knock the opponent out, or have more health when time runs out. Blocking cuts damage, but tackles and the finisher go through blocks, and low kicks and axe kicks still do half damage. Crouch to duck high attacks. Jump over low ones.
